Xiaomi Camera Native

Direct camera video for Apple Home · by Kosztyk

Add cameras to Apple Home

Scan each camera's QR code in Apple Home, or choose Add Accessory → More Options, select its name and enter its code.

Reading camera pairing details…

After changing your camera selection, save and restart Homebridge, then refresh this list. Each camera is added separately.

Xiaomi account

Choose the same region as Mi Home. Your camera video travels directly over your local network.

Use your account passToken, such as the one used by your existing integration. An individual camera's 32-character device token cannot be used here.

The password is used for sign-in only. Reusable account credentials are saved in a private file on your Homebridge host.

Cameras

Choose cameras to publish. Set the local IP if Xiaomi did not provide it.

Each camera is paired separately in Apple Home after restarting Homebridge. Its code and QR code appear in the pairing section above.

Video and controls

Live video and snapshots use H.264 conversion. This release provides video only; audio and HomeKit Secure Video recording are unavailable.

After saving, restart Homebridge to apply account and camera changes.
